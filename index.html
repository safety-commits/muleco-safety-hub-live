Recognition Categories

Elite → High Performer → Solid Performer → Average → Needs Improvement. The Performance Score is weighted 40% tenure-adjusted preventable accident performance, 40% service, 10% mileage-adjusted VEDR, and 10% safe-mile exposure. Higher mileage provides modest recognition for demonstrated safe exposure, but does not overpower safety or service. Non-preventable accidents and camera-equipment alerts are excluded.
